How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Rezx app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to your Account or Profile Settings.
  3. Select the Delete Account option.
  4. Follow the instructions and confirm your request.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Once your account deletion request is processed, your account and associated personal data will be deleted, except for information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

Request Account Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us:

Email: support.rezx@gmail.com

Please include your registered account email so we can identify your account.

Data Deletion Information

Some information may be retained when required by applicable laws or for legitimate legal purposes. Any retained data will be handled according to our privacy policy.
